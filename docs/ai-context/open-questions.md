40. Should the Email loop stop when no search is enabled? Since D37 (TD-13), search files of which none has `autoRun: true` are left as they are and nothing is searched; the Email loop then writes the warning once and checks 0 searches every minute ("Check N: 0 searches, nothing new."), because that is what every output type does with no search. 42. How long should an interactive search wait before trying a month's request again? GS1 (D45, business-rules §19) uses the console's waits (30 seconds after a failure, 1 second after heavy traffic), cancellable, with at most three requests per month in all, so a month whose requests all fail takes at most three 30-second request timeouts and two 30-second waits (about two and a half minutes) before it is reported as not checked; with the network down it is the two waits. No repository evidence supports other values (the R5 prototype measured cancellation, not recreation.gov's tolerance), and recreation.gov's rate limits are unknown (#14). GS2 (the GUI's Results tab) shows the retry in its progress; whether a window should wait less (and whether recreation.gov tolerates that) is a product decision for GS2, with its own approval.
